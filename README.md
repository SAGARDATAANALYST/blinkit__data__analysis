# 🛒 Blinkit Sales & Performance Analysis Dashboard

> An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, customer behavior, outlet efficiency, and product distribution using business intelligence techniques.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze Blinkit's grocery sales data and transform raw business data into meaningful insights using Power BI.

The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor sales performance, customer ratings, inventory distribution, and outlet performance while supporting data-driven business decisions.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Blinkit operates through multiple outlet types, locations, and product categories. Business managers require an interactive dashboard to answer questions such as:

- Which outlet types generate the highest revenue?
- Which products contribute the most to total sales?
- Which outlet size performs the best?
- How do customer ratings vary across products?
- Which locations should receive greater business focus?

This dashboard provides answers through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Blinkit generated over **$1.20M** in total sales.
- Low-fat products contribute significantly to overall revenue.
- Fruits, Vegetables, and Snack Foods are the highest-selling product categories.
- Tier 3 locations outperform other location tiers in total sales.
- Medium-sized outlets generate the highest revenue.
- Supermarkets contribute the largest share of total sales.
- Grocery stores maintain better product visibility despite lower revenue.

---

# 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Expand medium-sized outlets in Tier 3 cities.
- Increase inventory for top-performing product categories.
- Promote low-performing categories using targeted campaigns.
- Improve customer ratings through quality improvement initiatives.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing outlet types.
